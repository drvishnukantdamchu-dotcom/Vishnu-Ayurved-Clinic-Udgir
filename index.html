FAQ

Before Your Visit

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-in consultation may be possible, but calling or WhatsApping before your visit is recommended, especially for Panchakarma procedures.

Is Panchakarma the same for every patient?

No. Panchakarma is selected after assessment and depends on the patient, diagnosis and clinical suitability.

Should I stop my existing medicines before consultation?

No. Do not stop prescribed medicines on your own. Bring your medicines, prescriptions and recent reports.
