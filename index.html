FAQs

Before Your Visit

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-in consultation may be possible, but calling or WhatsApping before your visit is recommended, especially for Panchakarma procedures.

Is Panchakarma the same for every patient?

No. Panchakarma procedures and preparation depend on the patient's condition, age, strength, Dosha assessment and clinical suitability.

Should I stop my existing medicines before consultation?

No. Do not stop prescribed medicines on your own. Bring your current medicines, prescriptions and recent investigation reports for review.

Which branch should I visit?

You can choose the Dolphin Statue / Shelhal Road branch or the Krantinagar / Nideban Road branch according to the listed consultation timings.
